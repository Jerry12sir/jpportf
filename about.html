About Sathya

Telecom & Network Engineering student at CADT and Japanese IT pathway student at AUPP.

Bridging Technology & International Horizons

I am currently pursuing a bachelor's degree in Telecommunications and Network Engineering at the Cambodia Academy of Digital Technology (CADT), with an expected graduation year of 2027. My technical foundation centers on network infrastructure, protocol architecture, routing/switching, and IoT system implementation.

Moreover, I have a deep interest in Japan—its engineering rigor, technological precision, and culture. Driven by a desire to speak and communicate fluently like a native Japanese professional, I enrolled in the intensive Japanese IT pathway program at AUPP. Through daily language training combined with technical engineering studies, I am preparing myself for international teamwork and IT opportunities in Japan.

I am constantly seeking challenging opportunities, internships, and collaborative projects where I can apply my analytical skills, dedication, and engineering knowledge to build a bright future.
